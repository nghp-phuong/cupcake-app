Welcome home to a little seaside bakery.

Mai has come back to her grandmother's town to take over the family bakery. Open the doors, take orders, bake with your own hands and turn a quiet shop into the sweetest corner of town.

HOW A DAY WORKS
Each day brings a handful of guests. Read the order, then bake it step by step through six hands-on stations:
- Mould: drag the right mould or base into place.
- Mix: swirl your finger around the bowl for the right number of turns.
- Bake: press and hold, then release inside the golden zone.
- Frost: trace the guide to cover the treat with cream.
- Decorate: drag the requested toppings onto your bake.
- Box: pick the right box for the order.
Then serve it, collect coins and tips, and earn up to five stars.

HONEST, FRIENDLY FEEDBACK
After every order your guest tells you which step was great and which one needs work, such as "A little burnt!". You always know what to improve next time.

GROW YOUR SHOP
- 10 treats to master, from vanilla and chocolate cupcakes to macarons, cheesecake and layered birthday cake.
- Shop levels 1 to 15 unlock new recipes, toppings and more guests.
- Upgrade your oven through 5 levels for a more forgiving golden zone.
- Improve your whisk, piping bag and tongs to make every step easier.
- Decorate your shop with 5 decor slots. Each piece adds a little extra to your tips.
- Fill your recipe book by earning up to 3 stars of mastery on each treat.

A LITTLE STORY
Meet Mai and the regulars: Mr. Hoa, Linh and Kenji. Chapter 1 unfolds scene by scene as your bakery grows.

KEEP COMING BACK
- 3 daily quests with coin and gem rewards.
- A 7-day login gift calendar.

MADE FOR RELAXED PLAY
- Landscape play, with touch controls.
- No energy bars and no waiting. Play a day whenever you like, about 3 to 5 minutes each.
- Your progress saves automatically.
- Available in English and Vietnamese, or follow your device language.

ALPHA TEST BUILD
This is an early closed test version. Some things are still simple or temporary, and your progress may be reset between test versions. Thank you for helping us make the bakery better.

Recommended for ages 13 and up.
